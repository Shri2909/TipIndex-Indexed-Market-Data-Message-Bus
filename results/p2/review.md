## Findings and resolutions

1. **Embedded NUL path mismatch, corrected.** Initial `Registry::validate_path` accepted `std::string("a\0b",3)` while the independent oracle and trace v1 rejected it. Registry validation now rejects embedded NUL before registration; `V06Registry.PathValidationAndLookup` and `V07Subscriptions.InvalidSpecsDoNotRegisterEarlierValidTerms` include the case. Normalization validates every term before interning any root.
2. **Externally retained lifetime anchor, corrected.** Initial compiled matching checked only weak-pointer expiration before dereferencing a non-owning registry pointer. A caller could hold the public identity anchor across registry destruction. `Registry::Lifetime` now exposes read-only `alive()`, cleared at destructor entry, and `CompiledSubscription::{matches,belongs_to}` checks that state before registry access. `V08Subscriptions.RegistryLifetimeSafeEvenWithExternallyRetainedAnchor` holds the anchor through destruction and checks safe matching/destruction. Copy/move subscriptions share stable heap state and hook destruction unregisters listeners.
3. **Exact reserve growth, corrected.** Initial singleton registry/oracle append reservations could force quadratic vector moves. Final registry topic/partition/filter storage and oracle record/item storage grow geometrically while all allocations still precede visible registration/publication commit. No new benchmark scope or performance claim follows from this review.

Reviewed preflight: registry stages the complete missing ancestor chain, all topic/partition/local limits, path-map nodes/storage and every affected observer's update before commit. Observer updates are deduplicated by object; changed bitsets are staged, and commit uses pre-reserved storage/noexcept moves/swaps. Tests include real u16 local ID 65,535, unchanged missing ancestors/filters after exhaustion, zero capacities, invalid kind/path, and an injected observer allocation failure before any logical registration commit.

Reviewed union behavior: normalization removes duplicates and terms covered by ancestor subtrees at segment boundaries; exact shallow roots remain narrow, depth-one subtrees set `wide`, and all filters remain ordered by partition ID. New topic hooks traverse ancestors and update the stable compiled state, including future partitions under a wide subtree. The randomized test reference splits paths into segments and does not call optimized matching/normalization helpers.

Reviewed oracle progress: positive batches check terminal/exact-upper completion before expiry; zero budgets do not advance state; full batches stop immediately after their last delivered DATA; exhaustion advances the completed snapshot's upper checkpoint even with zero output. Future empty snapshots avoid `after_seq+1` and provide no reusable checkpoint. Equal-valued events retain distinct sequences. The independent model has no registry/filter/ring/index includes or production arithmetic helpers. Its non-owning replay pointer follows the ordinary requirement that the oracle outlive its cursors; engine/Tail implementation remains later-phase work.
